About Me

A passionate engineer uniting hardware intelligence with modern software systems.

I am a B.Tech Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student at Mohan Babu University with an active, focused drive toward Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, and Embedded Systems.

My engineering journey is defined by curiosity and practicality. I believe in learning through execution: analyzing real-world problems and building end-to-end solutions, such as digital twin laboratory monitors and automated face-recognition attendance systems.
